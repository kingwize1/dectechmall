## What the mall is

An aggregated ecosystem. Independent parties set up storefronts or deploy microservices. Shared rails are meant to handle compute, licensing, and settlement so a storefront does not have to become the platform.

### Decentralized rails (intended design)

1. Compute and hosting — DePIN / distributed GPU clusters for training and inference.
2. Identity and licensing — smart-contract pre-clearance, proof-of-authenticity, and programmatic licensing (pay-per-call, subscription tokens, or perpetual licenses).
3. Settlement and escrow — multi-currency rails (crypto, stablecoin, and fiat ramps) with deterministic split-payments to creators and node operators.

### Storefront categories

1. Agentic Workflows and Micro-Agents — autonomous lead-gen orchestrators, sales copilots, voice concierge endpoints, conversational UI bots.
2. AI Media and Creative Toolkits — proprietary style models (LoRA weights), synthetic voice, music clearance and stem processing, generative video modules.
3. Enterprise and Financial Logic Engines — risk modeling simulators, tokenized asset calculators, smart audit validators, compliance checking nodes.
4. Developer and Infrastructure Tooling — low-code/no-code scaffolding, WebRTC modules, API bridges.

These are categories the mall is meant to hold. They are not a list of products currently for sale, and no vendor is named because none was supplied.

## Economic model (intended platform design only)

Do not publish these as live offers, token metrics, or prices. The site states no APY, market cap, amount raised, or token price.

1. Floor Space and Node Staking — vendors stake or hold memberships to list vetted apps and keep verified storefront status.
2. Transaction Fee / Protocol Take-Rate — micro-fees on API calls, contract executions, asset sales, and subscription licenses.
3. Deterministic Revenue Splits — automated affiliate and referral distribution among lead sources, the platform treasury, and creators.
4. Tiered Access Passes (VIP / Enterprise) — high-compute tiers, proprietary enterprise models, low-latency nodes.
