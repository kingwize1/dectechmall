# Visual directions

Three directions were drawn before any page was built. Only Direction 1 is implemented. The other two are recorded so the choice stays explicit.

## Direction 1 — ProSport Metallic Crest (IMPLEMENTED)

Derived from the supplied 3D mark: cyber blue, laser gold/amber, metallic silver, gunmetal, an orbital ring, stacked “Decentralized / Ai Mall / .com”, and the lower banner “DECENTRALIZED AI TECH MALL BY VFOX ISLAND TRUST”.

- Canvas: deep gunmetal and near-black navy (`#080b10`), with a faint circuit mesh and one slow orbital ring. No purple gradient, no neon wash.
- Palette: only colors taken from that mark. Cyber blue and teal for links and the inner network, laser gold for kickers, numbers, and the primary plate, bright silver for headlines, charcoal for plates.
- Type: Barlow Condensed, heavy and truly italic, for headlines, navigation, and bay numbers — franchise and broadcast energy. Literata for body copy, so the page reads like a championship program rather than a rounded startup template.
- Motifs: orbital rings, beveled metal plates, stadium-roster rows, a home/away split for founders and vendors, a ledger instead of a pricing grid. Edges stay sharp. Circles appear only as rings.
- Layout: asymmetric hero (slogan left, crest right), full-width rails, numbered bays, an engraved economic ledger. Not a centered stack of equal cards.
- Rejected inside this direction: glassmorphism, stock “AI brain” art, generic icon grids, and any second logo.

The crest used in the header, hero, and favicon is a vector lockup of that supplied composition (same wording, same metal colors, same ring and banner). The original raster file was not present on disk in this workspace, so the mark was rebuilt as vectors from the artwork that was shown, not swapped for a different symbol.

## Direction 2 — Bright Editorial Fund Prospectus (not implemented)

A paper prospectus, closer to a private placement memorandum than a sports crest.

- Canvas: warm newsprint `#f3efe6`, ink `#1c1915`, a single vermilion rule `#9e2b2b`. No metal, no dark stadium.
- Type: a high-contrast old-style serif for headlines (Fraunces or Newsreader) and a text serif for body. Numbers set as footnotes, not jersey plates.
- Layout: a narrow editorial column, a wide margin for section numbers, hairline rules, and a cover that looks like a bound fund book. The slogan would sit as a title page, small-caps running heads, and a contents rail.
- Mood: quiet, legal, daylight. It would suit a funding memorandum and would fight the supplied crest, which is nocturnal, metallic, and athletic. Not built.

## Direction 3 — Warm Multicultural Typographic Poster (not implemented)

A market-poster system that leads with language and color rather than a crest.

- Canvas: terracotta `#9c4a2b`, indigo `#1d2a4a`, cream `#f6efe4`, saffron `#e2a01b`.
- Type: a heavy poster grotesque mixed with a calligraphic accent for the slogan, set at broadsheet scale with overlapping lines. No photographs of people, no stock “diverse team” imagery.
- Motifs: arches, awning stripes, and a repeated wordmark, like a night-market broadsheet for founders across cultures.
- Mood: warm and public. It honors the multicultural founder story and would bury the orbital metal mark the brand already has. Not built.

## Decision

Direction 1 is the site. Every CSS file and page file carries that note at the top. Directions 2 and 3 stay in this document only.
