01 — Mission

Product-market fit starts here.

What we do is simple. We connect diverse MVP founders and tech teams to forward-thinking, open-minded liquidity providers: debt and equity funders, private or public. Founders get distribution, real critique, and a path to capital. Funders see working software instead of another static deck.

SVM, meaning one

Silicon Valley Mall

An open marketplace for capital and deal flow. The successor to a single gated corridor: founders from any community set up in the mall and meet capital in the open.

SVM, meaning two

Solana / Sovereign Virtual Machine

High-speed, low-latency settlement rails intended for agentic tech, programmatic syndicate allocations, and tokenized instruments, where and when the law allows.

  1. Founder layerSupply Closed-door hackathons, the $149 technical audit, and MVP demo clearance.
  2. Media engineArena Live streams, audience voting, interviews, and educational passes that create real buzz.
  3. Capital layerLiquidity Green-Light judges, sponsors, and syndicate allocators who watch execution live.

02 — Platform mechanics

Audit. Pitch live. Get green-lit.

The mall is designed to turn real technical diligence into a live event, then route founders who earn it to capital. These are the intended mechanics and prices of the platform as it launches.

  1. 01

    $149 Founder Technical Audit

    A low-barrier, zero-equity registration fee. It is designed to cover an automated codebase audit through SWARM360, security screening on local hardware, and clearance for a demo slot in a closed-door hackathon or main-stage pitch. Founders keep their equity.

  2. 02

    Live-streamed pitch arenas

    Pitch competitions become interactive broadcasts on DecentralizedSVM.com over WebRTC, syndicated to Twitch as a top-of-funnel channel. Owned player rails are planned so the arena does not depend on one streaming platform.

  3. 03

    $9.95 spectator and educational passes

    Viewers register for a live event to watch, vote in real-time audience polls, and learn from the critique. The audience is meant to act as a sentiment signal, not a funding decision.

  4. 04

    The $1B Green-Light Judge Consortium

    A planned panel of accredited judges, private credit syndicates, and institutional allocators, intended to represent up to $1 billion USD in combined green-light funding authority. Judges can issue letters of intent and commit funding on their own terms after their own diligence.

  5. 05

    2% syndicate matching fee

    When capital is placed through the mall’s matching, the platform is designed to earn a 2% success fee on the capital matched, instead of taking founder equity.

  6. 06

    $1/month referral membership

    Free to join, with an exclusive $1.00 per month referral membership for supporters who want to follow the arena and share it. Referral distribution is designed to reward the people who bring founders and viewers in.

04 — SWARM360 architecture

A multi-agent engine on local edge AI.

SWARM360 is the planned multi-agent architecture that runs intake, broadcast operations, and capital routing, with deterministic checks between agents and people making the final calls.

  1. 00

    AURA-Prime

    Master orchestration and routing. Directs founders, viewers, sponsors, and capital allocators across DecentralizedSVM.com to the right path.

  2. 01

    Talent Agent

    Founder intake and validation. Scans submitted codebases, runs KYC and compliance checks, collects the $149 audit fee, and schedules closed-door pre-screens or main-stage slots.

  3. 02

    Stream Agent

    Broadcast operations. Runs low-latency WebRTC and Twitch distribution, chat engagement, pass clearance, live audience polls, and sponsor overlays.

  4. 03

    Capital Agent

    Investor matching. Aligns founders with relevant debt, equity, or liquidity providers by stage, sector, and ticket size, and supports syndicate setup.

Local edge AI compute stack

Zero-egress founder IP protection

Founder code is meant to be audited on hardware the platform controls, not sent to commercial cloud models. The planned stack:

  • Unified-memory edge nodes AMD Strix Halo / Ryzen AI Max+ 395 class mini-workstations with 128 GB unified memory, sized for local quantized large-model inference and code review.
  • Compact Nvidia AI boxes Local CUDA acceleration for tensor pipelines and stream processing.
  • Mobile AI workstations GPU laptops for field streaming and on-site hackathon intake.
  • Cold storage and hardware keys Air-gapped, FIPS 140-3 class encrypted modules and fast local NVMe caches.

05 — The mall and its rails

One mall. Shared rails. Independent storefronts.

Beyond the arena, the mall is an aggregated ecosystem. Independent creators, developers, and agent architects set up storefronts or deploy microservices. Compute, licensing, and settlement sit on shared decentralized rails, so a storefront can offer an agent, a model, or an infrastructure module without becoming the platform.

  1. 01

    Compute and hosting

    Local edge nodes plus DePIN and distributed GPU clusters for training and inference. A storefront is designed to draw on shared compute instead of standing up one vendor’s private cloud.

  2. 02

    Identity and licensing

    Smart-contract pre-clearance, proof-of-authenticity, and programmatic licensing. Access is designed as pay-per-call, a subscription token, or a perpetual license.

  3. 03

    SVM settlement and escrow

    Low-latency settlement rails with multi-currency support for stablecoins and fiat ramps, and deterministic split-payments to creators and node operators.
