01 — Vision and architecture

One mall. Shared rails. Independent storefronts.

The mall is an aggregated ecosystem. Independent creators, developers, and agent architects set up storefronts or deploy microservices. Buyers move through one marketplace. Compute, licensing, and settlement sit on shared decentralized rails, so a storefront can offer an agent, a model, or an infrastructure module without becoming the platform.

  1. 01

    Compute and hosting

    DePIN and distributed GPU clusters for training and inference. A storefront is designed to draw on shared compute instead of standing up one vendor’s private cloud.

  2. 02

    Identity and licensing

    Smart-contract pre-clearance, proof-of-authenticity, and programmatic licensing. Access is designed as pay-per-call, a subscription token, or a perpetual license.

  3. 03

    Settlement and escrow

    Multi-currency rails for crypto, stablecoins, and fiat ramps, with deterministic split-payments to creators and node operators.

02 — Storefront bays

Four bays. Many vendors.

These are the high-impact categories the mall is meant to hold. They are bays for independent storefronts, not a catalog of products this site is selling today.

  1. 01

    Agentic Workflows and Micro-Agents

    Autonomous lead-generation orchestrators, sales copilots, voice concierge endpoints, and conversational interface bots.

  2. 02

    AI Media and Creative Toolkits

    Proprietary style models and LoRA weights, synthetic voice, music clearance and stem processing, and generative video modules.

  3. 03

    Enterprise and Financial Logic Engines

    Risk-modeling simulators, tokenized-asset calculators, smart audit validators, and compliance-checking nodes.

  4. 04

    Developer and Infrastructure Tooling

    Low-code and no-code scaffolding, WebRTC modules, and API bridges.

03 — Economic model

How the mall is designed to earn.

DecTechMall is designed to earn from floor space, usage, and splits. The four lines below are the intended platform design.

Floor Space and Node Staking
Vendors stake or hold a membership to list vetted apps and keep a verified storefront.
Transaction Fee / Protocol Take-Rate
Micro-fees on API calls, contract executions, asset sales, and subscription licenses.
Deterministic Revenue Splits
Automated affiliate and referral distribution among lead sources, the platform treasury, and creators.
Tiered Access Passes (VIP / Enterprise)
High-compute tiers, proprietary enterprise models, and low-latency nodes.

Intended platform design. This page does not state yields, token prices, amounts raised, or market caps.
